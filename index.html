Booking checklist

UPI payment readiness

When IRCTC reaches its payment step:

  1. Choose UPI if it is offered.
  2. Check the payee, amount, and booking details.
  3. Approve the request in your UPI app or payment interface.
  4. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP with this assistant.
This app does not submit bookings, bypass queues or CAPTCHA, force the payment page to load, or authorize payment.
Journey details and checklist are stored locally in this browser. Do not use a shared device for private information.
